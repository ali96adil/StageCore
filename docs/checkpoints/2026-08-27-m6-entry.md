## M6 purpose

M6 is the final **software MVP closure** milestone. It owns the remaining authenticated operator workflow and the security/product gaps required before intentional Stage-LAN operator use and before physical Raspberry Pi qualification.

Tracking: Issue #25.

## Sequencing decision

Issue #21 Raspberry Pi qualification is deferred until M6 is merged. The first full hardware pass should exercise M0–M6 as one product rather than qualifying a backend-only subset and repeating the deployment after the operator/security layer is added.

## Boundaries

M6 does not include Hardware Nodes, a native DMX engine, AI/Vision, cloud dependency, HA Hub clustering, public Plugin marketplace, distributed multi-operator authority, or final hardware qualification.

Passing M6 means the software MVP is operator-usable through its supported authenticated local interface. It does not by itself claim rehearsal-ready/show-ready hardware.
